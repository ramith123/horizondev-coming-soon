questions · answered

Fair questions.

What is Reach?

A pocket CFO: one app that keeps your budget, savings goals, debt payoff plan, cashflow forecast and monthly insights in a single ledger, with entry fast enough that you actually keep it up.

Does Reach connect to my bank account?

No, and that's deliberate. There's nothing to link and no sync connections to maintain. You track expenses without connecting a bank account: entries take about three seconds and recurring bills log themselves.

How fast is logging a transaction?

About three seconds. Type “12 lunch today” and Reach parses the amount, category and date. You can also speak it, scan a receipt, use a full form, or set it recurring so it logs itself.

Is Reach available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Reach is live on the App Store and Google Play.

Is Reach free?

Free to download on both stores. Current plans and pricing are shown in the app.
